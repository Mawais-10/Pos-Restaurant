Privacy Policy - Restaurant POS

This application ("Restaurant POS") is a desktop point-of-sale system used by restaurants for managing orders, inventory, and sales records.

Data We Access

With your permission, this app connects to your Google Drive account solely to store and back up your restaurant's operational data (orders, inventory, sales records). The app only accesses files it creates itself and does not view, modify, or delete any other files in your Google Drive.

How Data Is Used

Data stored in your Google Drive is used only for backup and restore purposes within the app. It is not shared with any third party, sold, or used for advertising.

Data Storage

All operational data is primarily stored locally on your device. Google Drive is used only as an optional backup location, fully under your control.

Revoking Access

You can disconnect this app from your Google Drive at any time via your Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).
